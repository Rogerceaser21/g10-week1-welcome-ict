// learning_intention

What we are learning today

We are learning to explain what you already think artificial intelligence is. We are also learning to use the routines this class runs on.

// next_two_weeks

The next two weeks: AI, and a game of Tetris

  • What AI actually is. It finds patterns in the information it was trained on and predicts what comes next. It does not think.
  • Good prompt against bad prompt. A vague prompt makes the AI guess. A detailed one hands it a blueprint. The line from this unit: "Rubbish in, rubbish out."
  • Research what Tetris actually is, so you know what you are asking for.
  • You get 10 to 15 minutes to attempt a Tetris build any way you like. New for Grade 10, and not how the younger classes ran it.
  • Then the one-shot prompt. One long, highly detailed prompt that produces a working game.
The tool is Google Gemini. Its rules slide is the first thing we do next block, before anyone signs in.

Nothing is built today. Nobody touches Gemini today.
